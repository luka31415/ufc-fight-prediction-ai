# UFC results — 2026-10-11

[German results report](UFC_Ergebnisbericht.md) · [Period summary](period_summary.csv)

This upload contains aggregate event and period results for the available **42-feature Production v1/beta artifacts**, not the 46-feature Sherdog Production v2. Individual model probabilities, fight-level ledgers and raw supporting snapshots are excluded.

The money totals are partial simulations: historical odds are missing for 58 potentially eligible fights in 2026, including one after the training cutoff. Saved pre-event picks, retrospective reconstructions and archived yearly walk-forward predictions are distinguished in the report.
